Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026 • Package: com.mwveliz.tawpwat

TAWPWAT: The Amazing Warp Post Way at Tawpwat is developed by Miguel Delgado Veliz as a 100% offline, privacy-respecting game.

1. Zero Data Collection

TAWPWAT does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, or usage analytics. The Android application does not request internet access.

2. Local Storage Only

Game progress, unlocked relics, language preferences, and settings are saved strictly locally on your device (user://savegame.json) and never leave your device.

3. No Ads or Third-Party SDKs

TAWPWAT contains no advertisements, no in-app tracking SDKs, and no third-party analytics services.

4. Accessibility & Children's Privacy

Optional Text-to-Speech (TTS) and handheld vibration run locally via your operating system. Because no data is collected, the app complies with global children's privacy standards (COPPA/GDPR-K).

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact: mwveliz@gmail.com
